Full Body Machine Session

1–2×/week, at least 48h between lifting days Hard sets: 3 if lifting once that week, 2 if twice RIR 1–2: stop with 1–2 clean reps left Tempo 3-1-1: 3s lowering, 1s pause at the stretch, 1s lift Progress: add a rep, then add weight
W
Warm-up
About 10 minutes, before any hard set
3 steps›
  1. 1 · Easy cardio 5 min

    Bike or rower at a pace where you can still talk. Goal is to raise body temperature, not to tire yourself.

  2. 2 · Mobility 3–4 min

    10 arm circles each direction · 10 bodyweight squats · 10 light cable face pulls (rope at head height, pull to your ears, elbows high) · 10 hip hinges (hands on hips, push hips back, flat back).

  3. 3 · Ramp-up set per muscle group

    Before the first exercise of each group, do 1 light set of 10 reps at about 50% of your working weight. The 2nd and 3rd exercises need no ramp-up. This set does not count as a hard set.

After a Saturday-morning HIIT, keep the cardio step to 2–3 minutes and still do the ramp-up sets.
